# Let's block Ansys France and Synopsys France's PSE

The "Plans de Sauvegarde de l'Emploi" the two companies forced on their employees are not backed by any objective economic justification. They are only profit driven.

We have already been through a first wave earlier this year, which was not any stronger in terms of justification. This second wave is either evidence that these PSE are out of touch with reality or admission that they do not fix the problem.

# First stop on November 5, 2026

Unions have called for a national strike on Nov. 5 ([read CGT's communiqué](https://www.cgtetat.fr/societe-959/mobilisations/article/5-novembre-appel-a-la-mobilisation-pour-les-salaires-les-services-publics-l)). Even though its motivations are not related to the PSE, it makes the strike simpler. And we can still add our own claims!

As explained on [service-public.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F117#accordion-chapter-btn-1) :

> To qualify as a strike, the movement must be followed by at least 2 employees.
>
> However, an employee may strike alone within the company under the following conditions:
>
>   Either the employee accompanies a strike call launched at the national level
>   Either he is the only employee in the company.

## In practice

On Thursday, inform by email the following people that you are joining the national call for strike:

  - Human Resources
  - the General Manager
  - CSE members
 
Note that this notice is not mandated by law, as written on [service-public.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F117#accordion-chapter-btn-4) :

> No, the employee does not have to inform his employer of his intention to strike.
>
> It is up to the employer to note the absence of the employee on the day of the strike and to ask him the reasons for his absence. However, the employee can inform his employer of his absence if he wishes.

However, since we want to add our own claims related to the PSE, we believe it would be beneficial that strikers signal themselves individually. We prepared a short [communiqué for that purpose](communiqué-fr.md).
